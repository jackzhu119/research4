# E4: Computational cost evidence

Archived immutable attempt IDs are the accounting unit. Per-attempt receipts and supervisor ledger totals are reported separately; cumulative lineage fields are never added again. Failed, interrupted and retried attempts remain in the table. Process wall-time sums differ from concurrent study elapsed time. MC20/100/500 rows pair archived seed/fold contexts but do not claim identical cache or hardware conditions.

The original worker records total CPU and fit counters; it does not exhaustively measure exclusive stage durations, peak RSS and model bytes. The fixed three-context/four-method supplemental benchmark is therefore required when remaining budget permits. New profiles distinguish cold start from actual resume and cannot reconstruct missing historical stage times. No score determines this decision.

Rowwise SNV depends on all612 alpha points. A final62-dimensional classifier is not evidence for a62-point acquisition protocol or a proportional reduction in instrument time.
