1. Scope, estimands and evidence levels
Keep the v2 application framework: the archived two-material 174/174 and four-material 331/339 results provide task context, not new independent validation. The executable population available for the new mechanism study is the same 300 Suanzaoren TRAIN specimens with paired 300x612 alpha/n matrices. Historical R0->R2, 855 mixed-material/seven-source records and its net42/48 Suanzaoren improvement must remain a separate analysis; do not connect that sequence to current300 MC/spacing controls as one ablation chain. Pre-register four questions: where paired prediction changes occur; sensitivity to ranking construction/coordinate coverage; conditional incremental value of retained n; and whether data prerequisites for chemical guidance exist. All current300 conclusions remain internal to a previously examined, one-batch-per-source collection.

2. Exploration versus frozen internal re-evaluation
Use existing results only to define hypotheses and candidate grids. Freeze input hashes, all seeds, splits, candidate grids, tie rules, random-block seeds, primary contrasts, task order and stopping policy before new outer results are inspected. Inside every outer training set, let repeated stratified inner3fold x2 choose all eligible K/components/SVM/retain-drop/prior-strength settings. A separate list of newly frozen outer5fold seeds may assess repeatability after that freeze, but call it frozen internal re-evaluation, not independent confirmatory validation: the specimens have already been examined. Save outer predictions to a sealed result store; progress logging and resource adaptation may use job completion, errors and CPU cost, not outer accuracy. True confirmation requires independently collected source batches and acquisition metadata.

3. Mandatory matched references and primary method grid
All methods use identical outer/inner IDs, per-row preprocessing definitions, training-fitted scaling and selection rules. Required strong references: full-alpha shrinkage LDA (raw/SNV inner-selected), full-alpha RBF SVM, full-alpha PLS-DA; add full-joint LDA/SVM as modality references when feasible. MC pools: SNV alpha, both-SNV joint and raw-n/SNV-alpha joint. Cross ranking effort MC20/MC100 and a predeclared MC500 convergence tier with identical sample-priority streams where appropriate; select PLS components {2,4,8} and K {31,62,123,184,245,367,490,612} internally. Include fixed-components2 versus inner-components at fixed K62, and fixed K245 as a second anchor. Label-free uniform, contiguous-bin means and random subsets use the same widths and SVM grid. Bin means are derived predictors, not original selected channels. Use a fixed list of random subset seeds, never the outer-best subset. For spacing, use K62 with minimum index gaps {1,2,4,8}; K123 with {1,2,4}. Avoid infeasible combinations (e.g. gap8,K123 in612 channels). Report fixed-gap contrasts separately from an inner-selected-gap policy, because they answer different questions and have different search flexibility.

4. Mechanism controls beyond score comparisons
To study candidate-pool effects with equal final information, rank using [alpha,true n], [alpha,training-permuted n], and matched distractor/duplicate-alpha blocks, but retain exactly K alpha predictors for the final classifier. Define this as a ranking-only block diagnostic, not ordinary joint fusion. A global train-row permutation of n destroys specimen/label pairing; a within-class training permutation retains group-level differences while altering paired covariance. Keep their seeds fixed and construct them afresh only inside the relevant training subset. Validation n must not enter a training permutation or ranking fit. Include ordinary alpha-only ranking. These controls distinguish changes associated with pool width, class-linked block structure and specimen pairing while final alpha width is matched. They still do not provide chemical causation. In parallel, make retain/drop-n a genuine inner-selected option, then outer-evaluate that complete policy. Keep the existing post hoc fixed-C/numerical-gamma deletion diagnostic as a separate conditional result; do not promote its94% to a new selected primary model.

5. Outcomes, localization and statistics
Predeclare a small primary contrast set: component-selected versus fixed2 at K62; gap4 versus gap1 at fixed2/K62; both joint pools versus alpha under the same expanded protocol; and training-selected retain/drop versus always retain. Include full-LDA comparisons regardless of outcome. Pool each fivefold repetition over the same300 unique IDs and report paired specimen-level mean correctness, macro-F1, balanced accuracy, per-source confusion changes, corrected/damaged ID sets and recurrent ZY07->ZY04 errors. Summarize selected modality membership, native-coordinate coverage/runs, within-method Nogueira/Jaccard and between-method overlap. Do not use repeated predictions/folds as additional specimens. Conditional specimen-cluster intervals retain all repeat predictions and shared draws across contrasts; also report repeat-to-repeat variation. Explicitly retain training-set dependence and omitted batch/refitting uncertainty. Avoid uncorrected significance shopping; any formal multiplicity procedure and contrast family must be frozen in advance. A high or low outer score is never a stopping or extension trigger. Geometry measurements, including effective rank and neighboring correlations, describe frozen subsets; a spacing gain is not by itself evidence that reduced collinearity caused the improvement.

6. Chemistry feasibility and honest priors
The available dataset has no paired HPLC/compositional assay, independently validated within-window reference bands or solid-state chemical assignments. Some molecular calculations have imaginary modes and the lowest positive protocatechuic-acid mode at2.20786THz lies outside the nominal0.1-1.5THz interval. Therefore the present run must not manufacture a chemical-prior experiment or label a structural spacing/smoothing constraint as chemistry-guided. It can record the missing prerequisites and define an input contract for future measured chemistry: record_id-linked composition/assays, moisture/thickness, reference-compound and mixture spectra, solid-state/preparation validation and assay-batch provenance. A future prior should learn spectral-chemical associations only on each training subset and use bounded soft band/group weights with lambda=0 included; compare with no prior and random priors matched for weight distribution, smoothness/support and retained width. Outer assays can validate associations but must not be used to build that fold's prior. New-batch evaluation remains the eventual confirmation step.

7. Extensible genuine-training queue and resource fairness
Generate a sufficiently long deterministic queue (e.g. up to100 outer-repeat seeds with5folds, across the above reference, MC20/100 and MC500 convergence tiers) so the finite budget, rather than a tiny job list, bounds computation. More repeats estimate algorithmic repeatability on300 records; they do not add independent samples. Use a small resource-only pilot to estimate per-family CPU costs, without reading outer scores. Freeze a balanced core allocation first, then predeclared extensions in round-robin order: additional matched repeats, MC convergence, ranking-only block controls, and fixed random-subset/prior-control seeds. Cache rankings only within their own training allocation; reuse them across K/C/gamma without repeating identical fits to consume budget. Preserve negative/failed outcomes with receipts. Report paired results on completed common method/repetition blocks and list unfinished work explicitly; do not compare a cheap method's many completed repeats with an expensive method's truncated favorable subset.

8. Hard stop and four-worker execution boundary
The authorization is a maximum of90,000 aggregate CPU seconds, not a performance target or a demand to idle until a clock expires. Use at most4 workers and one numerical-library thread per worker; charge parent/child computation to the same global ledger. Choose the next complete comparable block from the frozen order only when the remaining budget can plausibly accommodate it, checkpoint at small deterministic boundaries, and stop dispatching at the cap. Keep a hard enforcement path for unexpectedly expensive work, retaining partial receipts for continuation rather than silently overshooting. An unused remainder smaller than a safe block is acceptable and should be reported. Neither accuracy targets, repeated outer inspection, elapsed wall time alone nor unlimited retry loops may extend the budget. The final report should include actual charged CPU time, completed/unfinished grid, all prespecified contrasts and explicit chemistry-prior feasibility status.
