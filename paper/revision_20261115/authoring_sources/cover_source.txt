# Cover letter

Dear Editor,

Please consider the manuscript “Tracing absorption information in terahertz discrimination of medicinal materials” for publication in Applied Spectroscopy.

The manuscript investigates terahertz fingerprints as a complementary approach to medicinal-material discrimination. Its spectroscopic contribution is to connect prediction performance to the signal variables actually retained and to the supervision used when additional labels become available. The collection contains 1,672 specimens representing Suanzaoren, Zaoren, Huangqi and Tianma. A common absorption classifier correctly classified 174/174 specimens in the two-seed-material comparison and 331/339 across the four materials.

The central representation analysis uses a balanced five-source Suanzaoren population with repeated nested validation and explicit final predictor budgets. At a budget of 62 variables, selection from a joint SNV absorption–refractive-index candidate pool achieved 87.11% accuracy, compared with 81.00% for SNV absorption-only selection. All 930 retained feature occurrences across the joint-pool outer refits nevertheless belonged to absorption. This result identifies a specific information pathway through selected absorption subsets and distinguishes candidate-pool integration from direct use of both modalities in prediction. Raw-absorption controls and the complete internally selected comparison are retained.

Additional controls separate valid support-label information from changes in standardization, kernel scale and total loss mass. The main five-source gain persisted with the source transformations frozen, supporting an explanation based on effective supervision and decision-function updating. Qualified PBE/def2-SVP protocatechuic-acid calculations provide a separate molecular frequency reference: both accepted models place their lowest harmonic fundamental above the terahertz modelling window. Full mode and infrared tables are supplied without assigning experimental herbal peaks from classification.

These results should interest readers working on spectral variable selection, data fusion and chemometric interpretation. The article presents a defined application across medicinal materials and a controlled account of how a compact spectral representation is obtained. Complete corrected positive and negative results, specimen roles, protocols and source receipts accompany the manuscript. The validation scope is internal to the represented collection.

OpenAI ChatGPT/Codex assisted repository inspection, archived-evidence checking, manuscript and language revision, and preparation and checking of analysis code. This assistance is disclosed in the manuscript.

The measured experimental data were provided by the corresponding author for the analyses reported here.
