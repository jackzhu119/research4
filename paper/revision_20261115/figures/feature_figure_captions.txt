Fig. 2. Effect of total retained predictor count on five-source Suanzaoren classification. (A) Mean accuracy across three pooled fivefold evaluations of the same 300 TRAIN specimens. The displayed methods use MC20 coefficient-stability selection followed by an RBF SVM, with eligible classifier settings selected within inner folds. K is the total final predictor count, including both blocks for the joint candidate pool. (B) Joint SNV candidate pool minus SNV absorption accuracy. Error bars are exploratory 95% percentile intervals from 5,000 specimen-cluster bootstrap draws (seed 20261109); resampling retains all three paired predictions for each specimen. Intervals are not multiplicity adjusted. The fixed-K analyses are sensitivity comparisons and do not replace the inner-selected primary results. Full results for all stored representations are provided in the supplementary data.

Fig. 3. Final predictor composition after joint SNV candidate selection. (A) Absorption and refractive-index occurrences across 15 outer refits at each total predictor budget. Fractions above bars give refractive-index occurrences divided by 15K. All 930 final predictor occurrences at K=62 belong to absorption. (B) Mean Jaccard overlap between the absorption coordinates retained by the joint-pool and absorption-only SNV procedures, calculated within each outer refit and averaged over the 15 refits. At K=62, the mean Jaccard index is 0.0111. Occurrences count predictor selections and are not independent measurements or distinct specimens.
